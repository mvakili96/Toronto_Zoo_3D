The new terrain package covers the Zoo, and the pilot is built and locally runnable. Twelve tiles from `elevation/GTA2023-DTM-10/` cover the entire saved 3 km × 2.5 km study area. The 2 m working mosaic has **1,875,000 valid cells and zero missing cells**. Terrain ranges from 90.19 to 159.22 m. The earlier `GTA2023-DTM-05` package has no Zoo overlap and is excluded.

Run `python3 scripts/serve.py`, then open **http://127.0.0.1:8000**. Rebuild and validation commands are in [README.md](README.md). Everything written by the app work is in `config/`, `generated/`, `public/`, `scripts/` and `reports/`; original input sizes and modification times were checked before and after preparation.

The rectangle in [study-area.json](config/study-area.json) is an **approximate study area**, including entrances, visitor parking, Meadowvale Road and immediate connections. No georeferenced official property boundary was supplied. A separate outline from [OSM way 229069541](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/229069541) is saved and shown as a dotted line. That mapped Zoo outline lies entirely inside the study area and is not a cadastral boundary. Maria’s imagery mosaic boundary is an image-coverage boundary, so it was not used as Zoo property.

| What I observed | What I decided | Evidence of the result |
|---|---|---|
| The first terrain package ended south of the Zoo. | Stop scene preparation until covering terrain was available; use your new package rather than inventing a surface. | [Tile index](reports/terrain-tile-index.json) lists all 651 inspected rasters and the 12 overlaps. Exact tile-footprint coverage and pixel checks both reach 100%. The earlier service terrain crop in `generated/sources/` is unused. |

New DTM tiles use compound EPSG:6660: NAD83(CSRS) / UTM 17N + CGVD2013 height. | Reproject horizontal positions into EPSG:2952 metres; subtract one saved local origin for the browser. Keep height values in metres. | Actual CRS definitions are saved in the [data audit](reports/data-audit.json). [Entrance, parking-access and pavilion overlays](reports/alignment-checkpoints.png) show buildings and routes on the City image. Visual review found no common translation to apply; this is a qualitative map check, not surveyed control. |
| Every selected Multipatch has minimum Z = 0. The footprint `SURF_ELEV` field is typically 0.40 m above the DTM, with a 10th–90th percentile range of 0.11–1.03 m. City metadata describes mean-sea-level/surface values without naming a vertical datum. | Treat Multipatch Z as local building height. Place each on the terrain, preserve its relative roof shape, and extend bottom wall vertices to ground. Do not silently apply a datum conversion or global 0.4 m correction. | [Grounding records](reports/building-grounding.json) expose source Z, terrain anchor and adjustments. Five small clearance lifts, from 0.057 to 0.838 m, prevent eave vertices being buried. [Data checks](reports/data-verification.json) confirm grounded base vertices and no building vertex below the intended 12 cm footing allowance. |
| Multipatch coverage crosses the pilot edge, and `Multipatch_60P/595` produces no usable non-ground triangles. | Keep complete usable Multipatches; use clipped City footprints at the edge and a footprint extrusion for the interior gap. | [Coverage audit](reports/multipatch-coverage.json), per-building identifiers, and the 67 explicit fallback records. No materials, façade colours, textures or windows were documented in the inspected fields; none are presented as measured attributes. |
| City generalized roads/pedestrian lines overlap the detailed OSM data but often represent different levels of detail. | Render OSM, retaining parallel paths; offer City geometry as a magenta comparison toggle. Remove repeated IDs and only truly coincident same-level copies. | City road-to-OSM distance has median 1.19 m but 90th percentile 60.81 m. The parking checkpoint shows separate OSM circulation routes against the image, while City routes are generalized. [Width records](reports/route-widths.json) identify the chosen method for every part. |
| Some ordinary road triangles can intersect a sloping terrain grid even when their corners sit on it. Sources also tag 18 raised crossing parts and six tunnel parts. | Adaptively subdivide road surfaces against the same terrain triangles, with a small display clearance. Build separate estimated bridge decks; omit tunnel interiors. | Browser sampling of 220,546 ordinary road/path triangle centres found zero buried samples, with 0.18–0.40 m clearance. Bridge estimates and source tags are listed in the [crossing audit](reports/data-audit.json). Deck lifts are 0.5–1.54 m and are not surveyed deck elevations. |

At the three checkpoints, terrain heights are 138.96 m at the entrance approach plaza, 134.36 m at parking access, and 150.03 m at the African Rainforest Pavilion. The image overlays and [review notes](reports/alignment-review.json) compare recognizable roofs and circulation. The listed City surface-minus-DTM differences are measured at the nearest building’s representative point, not necessarily at the checkpoint itself. No measured survey accuracy is claimed.

Browser verification used Chromium with software WebGL2. All 22 browser checks and 14 data checks passed. Orbit, pan, wheel zoom, reset, all eight main layer toggles, City comparison, image switching, source inspection and phone layout passed without JavaScript errors. Overview and close-ups were inspected and saved. The Seoul reference opened and created its WebGL canvases; its screenshot timed out in software rendering. The Zoo app’s screenshots succeeded. [Browser verification](reports/browser-verification.json) records the actual locally loaded resources: **10.32 MB initially, 13.59 MB with optional imagery**, including the page document. The aerial texture is loaded only when selected. The original inputs now total about 7.13 GiB and are never served to the browser.

Open issues are the unspecified City vertical datum, unsurveyed bridge deck heights, simplified pavilion roofs, the aerial’s unconfirmed capture year, and 41 route parts with short sampled grades above 25%. Those grades are flagged rather than flattened or reclassified without evidence; some may reflect narrow cuts, source alignment, or raised infrastructure that lacks a tag. Nine buildings span more than 3 m of terrain relief, including a pavilion spanning 14.3 m, so foundation appearance deserves further review. Grounding and centre-sample clearance tests do not certify every wall/road interior point or construction-level accuracy. Hardware performance and actual phone touch gestures still need device testing; this session checked phone layout and mouse controls in software Chromium.

For Toronto-wide work, reuse the CRS, coverage selection, source priorities, provenance and grounding checks. Replace the single scene bundle with spatially streamed tiles, multiple terrain/geometry resolutions, compressed meshes and tiled imagery. Index the full City geometry once, acquire the remaining terrain coverage, confirm a building vertical reference, extend the OSM context query, and validate more locations and crossings. The Zoo camera presets and labels also need to become data-driven. Keep a versioned source snapshot so service imagery and later OSM updates do not silently alter the result.

A later vegetation audit confirmed that the original way-only OSM query omitted two woodland multipolygon relations covering an additional **46.58 hectares**, mainly northeast/east of the Zoo. It also omitted **19 mapped individual trees and two tree rows** inside the study area. Sparse decorative placement and clearance buffers create additional gaps. The viewer remains unchanged; see [the investigation](reports/VEGETATION_AUDIT.md) and [comparison overlay](reports/vegetation-query-audit.png) for evidence and the proposed corrections. The original 5,110 rendered trees should not be interpreted as complete canopy coverage.

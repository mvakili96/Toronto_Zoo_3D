ABOUT THIS LANDSCAPE

Real sources.
Visible assumptions.

This is an exploratory model of the Zoo and its immediate surroundings. The rectangular study area is approximate; the dotted Zoo outline comes from OpenStreetMap, not a property survey.

The ground

Ontario GTA 2023 lidar terrain, CGVD2013 heights in metres. Displayed without vertical exaggeration.

The buildings

City of Toronto 2025 Multipatch geometry, with footprint extrusions where the 3D source has gaps. Colours are visualization styling. Click a building to inspect its source.

The routes

Maria’s Zoo OSM layers take priority; wider OSM layers supply surrounding routes. Widths are estimated from lanes or documented defaults. Parallel routes are retained. Bridge decks use estimated heights; tunnel interiors are hidden.

The image

Cached from Toronto’s open current-year ortho service on November 7, 2026. Capture year is unverified. Maria’s university-licensed 2022 imagery is excluded from browser assets.

Woodland shapes are mapped OSM areas; individual trees are decorative placement. Terrain is simplified to a 5 m grid. Building grounding is a visualization adjustment, not a vertical datum conversion.
